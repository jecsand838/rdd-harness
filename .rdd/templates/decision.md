# Architectural decision

> Authoring template. Give this decision a stable identifier under `docs/decisions/`. Accepted decisions are frozen solution artifacts for their run.

## Context

State requirement IDs, observable constraints, available baseline, and the decision that must be made.

## Options considered

Compare concrete viable options on behavior, complexity, dependency surface, compatibility, testability, and required operational consequences. Include extending the existing component where relevant.

## Decision and rationale

State the selected option, exact semantics or boundary it establishes, and why it satisfies the requirements. Distinguish author reasoning from sourced API facts.

## Consequences

List affected contracts/modules/scenarios, migration/upgrade effects, verification obligations, and limitations. Avoid creating new product requirements implicitly.

## Evidence and resolution

Link official sources and experiments. Record user direction when required, reviewer findings, date, run, and superseded decision if applicable.
